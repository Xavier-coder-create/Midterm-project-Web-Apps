Welcome to Kinematics and SUVAT equations

Suvat equations are a set of five equations that describe the motion of objects under constant acceleration. they can be used to solve for a unknown variable out of the five S(displacement), U(initial velocity), V(final velocity), A(acceleration), and T(time). ' These equations are particularly useful in physics and engineering to analyze the motion of objects in a straight line.
